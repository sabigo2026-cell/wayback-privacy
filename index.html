WakaBack Privacy Policy

Sabigo Connect Technologies · Last updated 31 October 2026

WakaBack records routes you choose to record so you can replay them and follow them back. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Location

WakaBack uses your phone's location only while you are recording a trip or using "Take me back". While either is running, the app may keep using location when the screen is off or you switch apps, and your phone shows a notification or indicator while it does. When you stop, WakaBack stops using your location.

Where your data is stored

Your trips (route points, times, stops and any voice recordings) are saved only on your phone. We do not run servers that receive your location, and we do not collect, sell or share it. WakaBack has no accounts and no analytics tools. To show adverts and the app's lettering, WakaBack downloads files from GitHub and Google Fonts. Like any website, these services can see your phone's internet (IP) address, but WakaBack never sends them your location, trips or recordings.

Sponsored messages

WakaBack may show sponsored messages from businesses on the Trips and Replay screens, marked "Sponsored". To pick messages for your country, the app reads the country from your phone's time zone and language settings. It does not use your GPS location for this, and nothing about you is sent to advertisers. Sponsored messages are never shown while WakaBack is guiding you. You can hide them with the ✕ button, and Premium members don't see them.

Sharing

If you tap Share or Share where I am, WakaBack writes a message containing that route and where it ends (or where you are now). It goes only to the people you choose, through SMS, WhatsApp or another app you pick. The route is stored in the part of the link after "#", which web browsers do not send to any server, so we never receive it.

Emergency contacts and SOS

Emergency contacts you add are saved only on your phone. When you hold SOS or choose to tell family you've arrived, WakaBack prepares a text message with your location for you to send from your own messages app. WakaBack never sends messages by itself, and we never receive your contacts or messages. WakaBack is not an emergency service: in danger, call 112.

Voice recordings

WakaBack records voice only if you turn on "Also record voice" before a trip, and only while that trip is recording. A red "Recording voice" sign shows the whole time. Recordings are saved only on your phone, are never uploaded or sent to us, and are not included when you share a route. Deleting a trip deletes its recording. Please tell the people around you when you record; recording people without their knowledge may be against the law where you are.

Unfinished trips

While you record, WakaBack saves your route on your phone every few seconds so it isn't lost if the app closes or the phone switches off.

Deleting your data

Delete any trip from the Trips tab. Uninstalling WakaBack removes all trips from your phone.

Children

WakaBack is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If a future version stores data online (for example, live tracking for family), we will update this policy and tell you in the app before it starts.
