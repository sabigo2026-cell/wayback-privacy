WayBack Privacy Policy

Sabigo Connect Technologies · Last updated 24 October 2026

WayBack records routes you choose to record so you can replay them and follow them back. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Location

WayBack uses your phone's location only while you are recording a trip or using "Take me back". While either is running, the app may keep using location when the screen is off or you switch apps, and your phone shows a notification or indicator while it does. When you stop, WayBack stops using your location.

Where your data is stored

Your trips (route points, times and stops) are saved only on your phone. We do not run servers that receive your location, and we do not collect, sell or share it. WayBack has no accounts and no advertising or analytics tools.

Sharing

If you tap Share, WayBack creates a trip code containing that trip's route. The code goes only to the people you choose to send it to, through the app you use to send it.

Deleting your data

Delete any trip from the Trips tab. Uninstalling WayBack removes all trips from your phone.

Children

WayBack is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If a future version stores data online (for example, live tracking for family), we will update this policy and tell you in the app before it starts.
